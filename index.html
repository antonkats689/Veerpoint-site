How is this different from an agency?
An agency works from outside and hands you meetings. A Veerpoint seller works inside your team, in your tools, and closes the deals.
Who owns the CRM, the contacts and the playbook?
You do. Everything sits in your systems from day one.
What do you need from us to start?
Time with whoever sells today, access to your tools (or a place to set them up), and someone who can join the calls that matter.
Can we hire our own seller later?
Yes. Everything is written down, so a new hire starts from a working setup, not from zero.
How is it paid?
A monthly retainer plus a share of what's closed. The rest is set on a call, around your market and deal size.
Where do you work?
Across Europe and the US. Engagements run in English; other languages depend on the seller.
