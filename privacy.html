Privacy.

Last updated November 2026

Who is responsible

Veerpoint runs veerpoint.io and is responsible for the personal data you send through it. For any privacy question or request, email hello@veerpoint.io.

What is collected

When you use the Book a call form: your name, work email, company and, if you add it, your phone number. When you email hello@veerpoint.io: what you write and your email address.

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. The hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and request time) to keep the site running and secure.

Why, and on what basis

Your details are used only to reply to you and arrange the call you asked for. The legal basis is your request: steps taken at your request before a possible contract. They are never sold, shared for marketing or added to a mailing list.

Who processes it

  • Vercel, which hosts the site and runs the form.
  • Zoho, which provides the email inbox your request arrives in.

Both act on Veerpoint's instructions. Some processing may take place outside the European Economic Area, under the safeguards these providers offer, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long it is kept

As long as needed to answer you and, if an engagement follows, for its duration. If no work follows, your details are deleted within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive a copy. Email hello@veerpoint.io. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
